Privacy Policy — Trustt Wellness App

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The Trustt Wellness app is published by Trustt Health & Wellness. This policy describes what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • An optional profile (age range, sex, life stage) used only to select the correct published reference table for your reports.
  • The supplements you add: names, brands, and the label values you confirm.
  • Photos of labels you photograph or pick, and the text read from them.
  • Your scan history and the reports you generate.
  • A record that you accepted the app's disclaimer, and your app settings.

This information is stored in databases encrypted on your device, and the app can be set to require Face ID, fingerprint, or your device passcode each time it opens.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. The app makes no network connections of its own: your profile, products, label photos, and reports are never transmitted to Trustt, to TechCAI, or to anyone else by the app.

The camera and your photos

The app uses the camera only when you choose to photograph a label, and reads your photo library only when you pick a photo. Label text is read on the device. Label photos are stored encrypted on the device and are never transmitted by the app.

Sharing is your action

Reports can be saved as PDF files generated on your device. When you tap share, your device's share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes. The app does not send the file anywhere itself. The same is true of the app's data export: the file is created on your device and leaves only through the share action you take.

Your controls

The app's Privacy Center shows a count of everything stored on your device. From there you can:

  • Export my data — one file with your profile, products, scans, and reports, plus a PDF of each report, shared only where you send it.
  • Delete all my data — removes your profile, products, label photos, scans, and reports from the device and returns the app to its first-run state. Copies you previously saved or shared outside the app are outside the app's control.

Who this app is for

The app is for adults. It asks every user to confirm they are 18 or older before first use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date, and material changes will also be noted in the app.
