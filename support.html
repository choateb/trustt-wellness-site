What the app does

Trustt Wellness adds up the supplements you take from their own labels. Photograph a label, confirm the values, and the app totals each nutrient across your products and compares the totals with the reference intakes published by the National Institutes of Health Office of Dietary Supplements. It produces a report you can view in the app and keep as a PDF. Everything happens on your phone: there is no account, and your information is never sent anywhere by the app.

This app does not check for interactions between supplements, medications, or health conditions.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and read the short introduction: what the app does and what it does not do.
  2. Confirm you are 18 or older and accept the disclaimer.
  3. Add your first product (below). A profile — age range and sex — is asked for only when you generate a report, because it selects the reference table the report compares against.

Adding a product from a photo

  1. On the Products tab, tap Add from a label photo.
  2. Photograph the front of the label, then the Supplement Facts panel — or pick photos from your library instead. You can skip the front photo.
  3. The app reads the panel on your phone and shows what it read. Compare every line with the label, fix anything that differs, and mark each line that matches. Only lines you mark are saved.
  4. Lines printed without an amount of their own — for example, ingredients inside a proprietary blend — are listed as not disclosed by the manufacturer and are not totalled.

Typing instead: on the Products tab, tap Add a product and enter the values from the label by hand. The same confirmation applies.

Seeing totals and getting your report

  1. On the Products tab, tap See my totals (also on Home).
  2. Set how many servings per day you take of each product. A blank entry counts as one serving per day, and the report says the servings were assumed.
  3. The report shows each nutrient's total, the reference intake, the Upper Limit (UL) where one has been established, and which products contributed what. Nutrients with no published reference value are listed as having no established reference value — they are never dropped silently.
  4. Tap Share or save PDF to hand the report PDF to the share sheet, where you can save it or send it wherever you choose.

Past reports are on the History tab and reopen exactly as generated. Scanning again creates a new report; an earlier report never changes.

Exporting or deleting your information

On the Privacy tab:

  • The counts at the top show what is stored on this phone.
  • Export my data builds one file with your profile, products, scans, and reports, plus a PDF of each report. It leaves the phone only through the share sheet, where you send it.
  • Delete all my data removes your profile, products, label photos, scans, and reports from the phone and returns the app to first-run setup. Files you saved or shared outside the app earlier are outside the app's control.

The app lock

The app can require Face ID, fingerprint, or your device passcode when it opens and when it returns from the background. The setting is on the Settings tab and is on by default. On a device with no passcode or biometrics set, the app notes that the lock is unavailable — your stored information remains encrypted either way. There is no separate app password, and no one can recover your stored information for you: it exists only on your phone.

Troubleshooting

  • "This photo could not be read clearly." Retake the photo in even light with the panel flat and fully in frame, or type the values in — the typed path reaches the same confirmation screen.
  • "That photo could not be saved on this phone." The phone could not store the photo — commonly because storage is full. Freeing space and trying again usually resolves it. Everything already saved is unchanged.
  • A nutrient is listed as not totalled. The label did not disclose an amount for it (commonly inside a proprietary blend), or its unit could not be converted in a way the published references define. The report gives the reason for each such line.
  • The report shows different numbers than you expected. Check the servings per day used for each product — totals multiply each product's per-serving values by the servings you entered.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app send my information anywhere? No. The app makes no network connections of its own. Files leave your phone only when you share them through the share sheet.

Why does the report ask for my age range and sex? Published reference intakes differ by age and sex. The profile selects the correct table; it is stored on your phone only, and it is asked for only when a report is generated.

Does the app cover food or prescriptions? No. Reports cover only the supplement products you scanned, as labelled. This app does not check for interactions between supplements, medications, or health conditions.

Is the report medical advice? This report is educational information based only on the product labels you scanned and reference intakes published by the National Institutes of Health. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a recommendation to start, stop, or change any supplement or medication. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your results.
